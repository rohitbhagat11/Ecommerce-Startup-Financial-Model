# E-commerce Startup Financial Model

A 12-month, monthly operating forecast for an e-commerce startup, built in Microsoft Excel. The model connects traffic and conversion assumptions to orders, revenue, variable costs, fixed costs, and projected profit, with charts for traffic sources and revenue versus costs.


## Model snapshot

| Metric | January 2022 | December 2022 |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Revenue | $2,124 | $24,278 |
| Gross profit margin | 46.0% | 74.4% |
| Profit / (loss) | ($1,928) | $8,944 |

The forecast shows monthly profit turning positive in July 2022. Revenue rises through the year as modeled traffic and orders increase. 

## What the model covers

- **Revenue build:** organic and paid page traffic, channel conversion rates, orders, average item value, items per order, and average order value.
- **Cost build:** manufacturing, order fulfillment, paid traffic cost, warehouse rent, office rent, salaries, and other costs.
- **Income statement:** revenue, cost of goods sold, gross profit and margin, variable costs, contribution margin, fixed costs, EBT, taxes, and profit / (loss).
- **Visualizations:** monthly organic versus paid traffic, and revenue versus total costs.

## Key assumptions shown in the forecast

- Forecast period: January to December 2022, by month.
- The workbook lists dollar-denominated assumptions and a 19% tax rate.
- Manufacturing cost per order falls from $13.50 to $11.50 beginning in June.
- Fulfillment is modeled at $5.00 per order; paid traffic cost is $0.40 per paid click.
- Revenue is driven by separate organic and paid traffic and conversion assumptions, plus average item value and items per order.
